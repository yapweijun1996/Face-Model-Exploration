Local face model exploration

Exploratory scores only. Intended identity labels are unverified and are not ground truth. No new-model match thresholds or probability claims. Prior Face-API scores are Euclidean distances; SFace, EdgeFace and AuraFace scores are cosine similarities. Detectors and preprocessing differ.

Score distributions

Quantiles summarize available scores only; counts may differ when detection or inference fails. Face-API distance is lower for closer faces; all other scores are cosine similarities, where higher is closer. Scores from different models cannot be directly compared.

Node.js model inference time

Milliseconds for the ONNX model call only, excluding image decoding, YuNet detection, alignment, and model loading. One run includes the first inference and is not a production benchmark. Do not compare these values with the older full-pipeline times.
